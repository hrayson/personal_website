abstract: >
  We used long-term observations of wild chimpanzees in Taï National Park to investigate
  how immature chimpanzees seek contact with their mothers. Their responses during naturally
  occurring threats supported the presence of organised patterns, including secure and
  insecure-avoidant attachment. We found no evidence for disorganised attachment in the
  animals observed.
