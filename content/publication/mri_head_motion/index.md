abstract: >
  This study developed an in vivo method to test MRI head-motion tracking against a common
  reference. Six adult volunteers made controlled head rotations in the scanner under visual
  guidance. We compared a markerless optical system and fat navigators against estimates
  from anatomical image registration, and assessed image quality after retrospective motion
  correction.
