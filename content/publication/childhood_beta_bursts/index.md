abstract: >
  We used EEG to examine how children with and without developmental coordination disorder
  recruit brief sensorimotor beta events while performing and watching gross and fine
  motor actions. The patterning of particular burst waveforms differed between groups,
  including how activity was distributed across
  hemispheres. Burst properties also varied with motor skill across the sample, helping to
  describe individual differences beyond diagnostic group.
summary: "Properties of distinct sensorimotor beta bursts tracked variation in motor skill across children with and without developmental coordination disorder."
