My research examines how parental mental health and early parent–infant interactions relate to infant brain and behavioural development. I use longitudinal studies combining observations of parent–infant interaction with measures of parental wellbeing and infant attention and brain development.

I also work with [LENA](https://masantementaleperinatale-lena.fr/nousConnaitre/quiEstLena), a partnership between clinicians, researchers and people with lived experience of perinatal mental health difficulties. I contribute to [parent-facing resources on early relationships and attachment](https://masantementaleperinatale-lena.fr/comprendre/attachement) and have co-developed psychoeducation materials on interacting with infants aged 0–6 and 6–12 months for use in clinical group sessions.
