I use EEG to study how brief bursts of beta-band activity in sensorimotor cortex change as children gain motor skills. We examine when bursts occur during action observation and execution, how their waveforms vary, and what that variation tells us about motor development.

Our [infant-to-adult study](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0886-23.2023) showed that changes in the rate of particular burst waveforms around movement become more precisely timed and more lateralised with age. In a [2026 study of children with and without developmental coordination disorder](https://doi.org/10.1162/IMAG.a.1376), the rate of particular burst waveforms during action varied with motor skill across both groups. Our [review of beta burst waveform diversity](https://doi.org/10.1177/10738584251390779) examines what these waveforms may reveal about the underlying neural circuits.
